# Opening field recordings

The short in-game ambience masters are edited excerpts of recordings hosted by
Wikimedia Commons:

- `en-city-morning.mp3` and `zh-night-city.mp3`: “Ambient sound street traffic
  Berlin 2026-05-17”.
- `ja-rain-train.mp3`: “Sound of a MoHa 201 series electric multiple unit train
  on the Chūō Main Line (Ochanomizu–Yotsuya), Tokyo, Japan - 20101010” combined
  with “Rain.ogg” (public domain).
- `es-night-plaza.mp3`: “Shopping mall less crowded.ogg”.
- `pt-ocean-sunset.mp3`: “Oceanwavescrushing.ogg”.

Refer to the corresponding Wikimedia Commons file pages for creator and license
metadata. The edits are trims, level balancing, fades, filtering and mono game
delivery encodes.
