# SUASION audio credits

Every sound in this build is synthesised at boot by `scripts/sfx.gd` (shaped sine tones;
the cue table is in `_ready()`): no recorded or third-party audio, so nothing to license.
The campaign's cues added for the Nutaku build -- `hit` (a card wears down her resolve),
`flourish` (a phase change; a rare or epic pull), `steal` (Celeste's cut) -- are made the
same way. Dropping an `.ogg` at `assets/sfx/<cue>.ogg` replaces a cue; credit it here.
