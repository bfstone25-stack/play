Understand complex medical documents. Catch costly pitfalls. 看懂医疗文书，避开昂贵的坑。

MediKin · 家医管家 is a bilingual family-health companion for making sense of paperwork: medical bills, lab reports, insurance EOBs, doctor's notes, and medication instructions. Choose whose document it is and the document type, paste the text, and receive a plain-language explanation — what it says, what looks abnormal, and what to ask next.

Free to use online — runs entirely in your browser, in 中文 or English. Pasted text is used for the current reading only and is never stored.

  • Five document types — medical bills 账单, lab reports 化验单, insurance EOBs 保险说明, doctor's notes 医嘱, and medication documents 用药.
  • Document-grounded — every claim in the explanation maps back to the source text you pasted.
  • Risk checks — flags abnormal lab values, inconsistent amounts, missing details, and easy-to-miss terms.
  • Next-question prep — walk into the clinic, pharmacy, or insurer call with precise questions ready.
  • Family-aware — readings framed for self, parent, spouse, or child.
  • Zero retention — documents are read for this session only, never stored.

Built for families navigating an unfamiliar healthcare system — especially bilingual households translating between medical English and 中文 at the kitchen table.

MediKin is informational only. It does not diagnose conditions or replace a licensed clinician, pharmacist, insurer, or emergency service.
