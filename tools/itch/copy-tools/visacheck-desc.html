VisaCheck · 签途审计 is a multilingual pre-submission audit workspace for U.S. immigration paperwork. Select your visa category and document type, paste your draft or key form data, and receive a sentence-level review that flags RFE-prone wording, logic gaps, and formatting issues — with concrete fixes.

Free to use online — the full audit runs in your browser in 9 languages. Your draft is used for the current audit only and is never stored.

  • Seven filing categories — H-1B, OPT / STEM OPT, EB-1 / NIW, EB-2 / EB-3 PERM, I-485 adjustment of status, F-1, and O-1.
  • Sentence-level review — flags vague claims, unsupported superlatives, and inconsistencies an officer would question.
  • RFE-risk focused — written from the reviewing officer's perspective, before the Request for Evidence arrives.
  • Concrete fixes — every flag comes with a specific rewrite or document to add, not just a warning.
  • 9 languages — English, Español, Português, Français, العربية, हिन्दी, 한국어, Tiếng Việt, 中文.
  • Zero retention — drafts are audited for this session only, never stored.

Built for applicants, petitioners, and HR teams preparing recommendation letters, employer letters, and personal statements — a final consistency pass before the attorney review or the mailer.

VisaCheck provides organizational and writing assistance only. It is not legal advice and does not replace review by a qualified immigration attorney.
