Not an answer imposed on you, but a light by which to see more clearly.

Logos is a quiet reflection companion grounded in Scripture, honest psychology, and compassionate conversation. Share what is weighing on you — anxiety about the future, a strained relationship, a question of purpose — and receive a thoughtful response with verified Scripture references you can check yourself.

Free to use online — no account, no download. Open the page, choose among 12 languages, and start the conversation.

  • Scripture-rooted — responses cite verified references, never invented verses.
  • Honest psychology — practical wisdom for belonging & self-worth, parenting & the next generation, cynicism & injustice, shame & relationships.
  • 12 languages — English, 简体中文, 正體中文, Español, Português, Français, Deutsch, हिन्दी, Bahasa Indonesia, 日本語, 한국어, العربية.
  • Private by design — a quiet space to think; nothing is asked of you beyond your question.
  • Installable — add it to your home screen and return whenever you need a moment of clarity.

Made for anyone carrying a difficult question — believers seeking grounded counsel, and the simply curious looking for a calmer way to frame a situation.

Logos offers companionship grounded in Scripture and practical wisdom. It does not replace professional mental-health, medical, or pastoral care.
