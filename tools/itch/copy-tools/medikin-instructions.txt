Use in the browser: click Run tool, then follow the two steps.

1 · Whose document is this? — choose self, parent, spouse, child, or other, then pick the document type (medical bill, lab report, insurance EOB, doctor's note, medication).
2 · Paste the document text — receive a plain-language explanation with abnormalities, inconsistencies, and suggested follow-up questions.

Switch between 中文 and English from the header. Pasted text is used for this reading only and is never stored.

Free to use. MediKin is informational only and does not replace professional medical care.
