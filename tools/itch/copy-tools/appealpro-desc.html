Appeal with structure and evidence, not emotion. 申诉靠结构与证据，不靠情绪。

AppealPro · 申诉专家 is a bilingual writing workspace for marketplace sellers facing platform enforcement. Choose the platform — Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Walmart, or Shopify — and the issue type, describe what happened, and receive a professionally structured appeal letter you can review, adapt, and submit.

Free to use online — the full bilingual workspace runs in your browser. Nothing you paste is stored: each draft is generated for this session only.

  • Five platforms covered — Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Walmart, and Shopify enforcement workflows.
  • Five case types — account suspension 封号, IP complaints 侵权投诉, negative reviews 恶意差评, A-to-Z claims 索赔, and listing removals 下架.
  • Policy-aligned drafts — letters follow each platform's review logic: root cause, corrective actions, preventive measures.
  • Measured, professional tone — facts and evidence only; no emotional language that weakens a case.
  • Bilingual by default — full 中文 / English interface and output for cross-border sellers.
  • Zero retention — case details are used for the current draft only, never stored.

Built for cross-border sellers, store managers, and e-commerce operators who need a clear first draft under time pressure — before deciding whether to escalate to a consultant or attorney.

AppealPro provides writing assistance, not legal advice, and does not guarantee an appeal outcome.
