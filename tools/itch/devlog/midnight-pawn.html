Midnight Pawn & Crypt is live — a pixel shop-keeping RPG with a mini dungeon, free slice in the browser.

You are Nara Quill, heir to a pawn shop that restocks only at midnight. Every object has two prices: what the living offer, and what the dead demand.

Play the free slice in your browser — the tutorial sale, Day 1 customers, and the full Night 1 descent: two crypt rooms, extraction, and the ledger that remembers everything. Mouse, touch, and keyboard all work.

The complete edition (Windows / Linux / macOS) finishes the run: Day 2 customers Tamsin and Ivo, the Ossuary Market, the Foreclosure Chapel, the optional relic, and the final appraisal of the Heart of the Crypt — sell it, seal it, or keep it.

  • One deterministic run, 15–20 minutes — no countdowns, no energy gates
  • Exact integer economy; a run can never softlock
  • Three endings, score ranks S–D
  • Five languages: English · 简体中文 · 日本語 · Español · 한국어

Made with Godot. The lamps stay lit for paying customers.
