A pixel management RPG. By day you weigh prices. By night you weigh the cost.

You are Nara Quill, heir to a pawn shop that restocks only at midnight. Aunt Elsa's will warns you: every object has two prices — what the living offer, and what the dead demand. Appraise cursed curios by day. Descend into the pocket crypt beneath the shop by night to reclaim what the dead sold.

Read exact values, curses, and demands. Price LOW, FAIR, or HIGH. Warn buyers or hide the curse. Carry one curio into each night and risk only what you have not banked — until the final appraisal of the Heart of the Crypt: sell it, seal it, or keep it.
