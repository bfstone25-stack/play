Place staff. Build chains. Pay the landlord.

OFFICE LANDLORD is a desk-floor strategy toy. A 5×4 floor, three offers each beat, and rent that does not care about your feelings. Place each piece yourself — interns, headphones, programmers — and chain them into revenue before the landlord collects.

Free to play online — the full lease runs in the browser, eight floors deep.

  • Draft and place — three offers per beat; where you put them is the whole game.
  • Chains pay — adjacent synergies multiply the take. Empty floors are not a strategy.
  • The landlord watches — rent scales every floor; miss it and the lease ends.
  • Relics and settlement — collect bonuses, then COLLECT SETTLE to bank the floor.
  • Three languages — English, 中文, 日本語.
