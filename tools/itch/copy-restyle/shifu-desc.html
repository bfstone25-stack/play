ShiFu · 找师傅 helps North American Chinese-speaking households explain home-service needs, discover suitable professionals, request quotes, and manage conversations in one bilingual workspace. Homeowners can browse living inspiration and organize a job request; professionals can review demand, availability, and business activity.

Free to use online — the full bilingual workspace runs in the browser, for both sides of the marketplace: 我是客户 (for home) and 我是师傅 (for business).

  • Bilingual by default — 中文 / English throughout, built for Chinese-speaking households and the pros who serve them.
  • For homeowners — living inspiration, job requests, quotes, and conversations in one place.
  • For professionals — demand, availability, orders, and business activity at a glance.
  • Installable — works as a browser app; add it to your home screen.
