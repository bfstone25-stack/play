He remembers you. Every word, every coffee order, every 2 AM thing you said once.

FLUTTER is a live-AI romance RPG. Six men, five languages, one rule: nothing is scripted twice. You talk, he answers — a real reply, written for you, in character, remembering what you told him three conversations ago.

Free to play online — the full browser edition runs here: opening cinematic, character select, live conversations, a theme song per language. No install, no account, no paywall at the door.

  • Live LLM conversations — not a dialogue tree. He reads what you type and answers in character.
  • He actually remembers — details you mention come back later, unasked.
  • Six routes — Adrian, Ethan, Liam, Fu Shen, Gu Yan, Lu Xingye. Different tempers, different cities.
  • Five languages — English, Español, Português, 中文, 日本語, each with its own opening film and theme song.
  • Cinematic openings — a short film before every route sets the morning, the rain, or the train.

Best with sound on. Headphones after dark.
