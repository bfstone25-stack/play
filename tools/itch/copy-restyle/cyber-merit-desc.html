Free to play online — the full shrine is in the browser. Bilingual (中文 / English) from the first tap.

  • Combo and charge — rhythm taps fill the mandala; 36 merits settle the day.
  • LING-7 — a small shrine robot with opinions about your technique.
  • Short sessions — sixty seconds of tapping counts as practice.
