# Crazy Rant — itch copy (office-politics shmup)

Short:
Office-politics bullet hell. Workplace knives, written as ofuda.

Description:
Crazy Rant is a shmup. The boss is the meeting. The bullets are shrine ofuda — tall paper charms, not horizontal sentences.

Every incoming shot is a short office phrase, vertical ink on vermillion-bordered washi. Weave the lanes. Graze the text. Fire your own rant back. EN / 中 / 日.

Arcade is four meetings: Standup → 1:1 → All-hands → PIP. Survive PIP and the cabinet opens Endless Overtime — rank climbs, patterns shuffle, no two alike in a row.

Keyboard or drag to dodge. Auto-fire. Tiny hitbox.

This is not a typing game.
