# Crazy Rant — itch copy (office-politics shmup)

Short:
Office-politics bullet hell. Workplace knives, written as bullets.

Description:
Crazy Rant is a shmup. The boss is the meeting. The bullets are the words.

Every incoming shot is a readable office phrase — CC the lead, let's sync, not personal, overtime is growth. They arrive as cards, stamps, emails, rings, spirals, boomerangs, splitters, wall curtains, and slow giant "blessings." Graze the text. Fire your own rant back.

Arcade is four meetings: Standup → 1:1 → All-hands → PIP. Survive PIP and the cabinet opens Endless Overtime — rank climbs, patterns shuffle, no two alike in a row.

Keyboard or drag to dodge. Auto-fire. Tiny hitbox.

This is not a typing game.
