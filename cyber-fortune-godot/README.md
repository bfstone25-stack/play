# 赛博求签 / Cyber Fortune — Godot 4.7

The playable prototype of `ops/adult_forks/cyber_merit_fortune.md`: three instruments,
one machine. Series 情绪解药 / Emotional Catharsis (`play/catharsis/PLAN.md`); mainstream,
SFW. Replaces the merit clicker in `play/cyber-merit/` (kept; Blaze decides).

Blaze's 2026-09-18 decision — every title through a game engine — overrides PLAN.md's
"Vanilla JS + Canvas 2D" lock. Everything else in PLAN.md is kept: portrait 720×1280,
copy in JSON, `user://` persistence only, commerce stubs that grant locally, no backend.

## Run

```
GODOT=~/bin/godot/Godot_v4.7-stable_linux.x86_64
$GODOT --headless --path . --import      # once, and after new files
$GODOT --path .                          # desktop; space taps the fish
bash tests/run.sh                        # 83 headless checks; fails on any engine error
./build.sh                               # pools + CJK subset, then web/linux/windows -> ../../build/godot/cyber-fortune/
python3 tests/headless_web.py            # drives the web build in Chromium, screenshots -> shots/
```

## What is playable

| | |
|---|---|
| **求签, the slip tube** | Tap the wooden fish (merit.js curve, ported exactly), the lacquer band rises; at 100 the tube shakes — sticks rattle, one rises and falls out — and the slip is read on paper: rank, four-character 签文, plain reading, one thing to do today. Six subjects. Keep / burn / 化解. |
| **The deck** | Cut, turn one of the 78, upright or reversed, in one of four positions. Procedural card back and a typographic face; art slots in later as `assets/tarot/<slug>.webp` by filename, nothing else changes. Keep / burn / integrate. |
| **读心术, the reader** | Four forces, each a scene at her table with a beat before the reveal, then a line she gives back: the six-card binary force (any 1–63), the mathematical force (always the lantern), the Princess card trick (yours is gone), and an equivoque (always the candle). The door says once, lightly, that she performs. |
| **The rack** | One collection for both: tied 凶 / reversed cards converting on a real-time delay (20 min / 60 min) into +1 merit per tap; the slips by rank × subject; the 78 with their reversals. |
| **Offerings** | The four SKUs, mocked; "a paid draw is never a luckier draw" is the first line. |
| **Shared** | One merit currency (named *focus* on the West side), one free draw a day per account, pity ladder, `user://fortune.json`, zh-Hans / en toggle, the casual board offered after the daily read via `Gate`. |

## Numbers

- **Slips: 150** — 6 ranks × 6 subjects, 4 per cell + 6 extras. Hand-written in
  `tools/pool_slips.py` (not generated: writing was faster than driving the llama-server
  and reviewing its classical Chinese). The spec's launch target is ~600; this is a quarter.
- **Tarot: 78 cards, 156 readings** (upright + reversed, each with a do-line), zh + en, in
  `tools/pool_tarot.py`. The Majors are individually written; the Minors are written per
  card (56 upright + 56 reversed), shorter.
- **Ladder** (`scripts/fortune.gd`): 大吉 3 / 中吉 12 / 小吉 25 / 末吉 30 / 凶 22 / 大凶 8,
  gacha.js's pity (10th draw without 中吉+ forces one). Cards: rank → tier and orientation
  (`RANK_TIERS`); every one of the 156 is reachable. Measured over 30 000 seeded rolls in
  the tests: 大吉 2.8%, 中吉 15.7% (lifted by pity), 大凶 7.8%.
- Rack: 3 slots; 化解 costs 40 (凶) / 80 (大凶) merit; burn returns 60/40/25/15/10/10.

## Verified by running

- `tests/run.sh` — 83 checks: 648 steps of clicks/ticks/upgrades/loads identical to
  merit.js (`tests/merit_conformance.json`, regenerated by `node tests/merit_conformance_gen.cjs`);
  the ladder's shape and the pity force over 30 000 rolls; free draw / 100-merit cost /
  keep / burn / extra_draw; the timed 化解 with a moved clock, the +1 bonus, the rack cap,
  `resolve_now`; the binary force for all 63 numbers, the math force for −200…2000 and
  halves, the Princess set, all 12 equivoque paths; pool sanity; JSON round-trip.
- `tests/headless_web.py` — the real web export in headless Chromium through the whole
  loop, 29 screenshots in `shots/`: free draw → keep → the casual board (and only then);
  a paid draw; a 凶 tied, ready after the clock moves, claimed; a card turned and kept
  into the same collection; all four forces revealing the predicted answer; the shop
  grant; zh; phone width; reload persistence; no page errors, no popups.
